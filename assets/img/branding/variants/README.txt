SUSUME TECH - LOGO VARIANTS
===========================

Generated from ../Logo.svg (the master). Every file is a standalone SVG with a PNG preview next to it.

LAYOUTS
  primary/      Full logo: cloud, walking figure, tech-icon "dust", circuit traces, wordmark.
                Use large: website hero, presentations, posters, large print (wider than ~8 cm / 300 px).
  simplified/   Cloud, figure, 6 bold icons, wordmark. No traces; the cloud's left edge breaks up into pixels.
                Use small to medium: business cards, letterheads, site header, merchandise, email signatures.
  icon/         Cloud and figure with a pixel-breakout left edge (no icons), square canvas.
                Social avatars, app icons, favicons.
                favicons/ contains favicon.ico (16/32/48) and PNGs at 16, 32, 48, 180 (Apple touch), 192 and 512 (Android/PWA).
                Note: at 16-32 px the figure is too thin to read, so it shows as a blue cloud. Use 48 px or larger where possible.
  wordmark/     "SUSUME TECH" only. Narrow horizontal spaces, footers, co-branding next to partner logos.

COLOUR MODES (each layout has all five)
  _color   Blue-cyan gradient. Default on white or light backgrounds.
  _dark    For dark backgrounds such as susume.tech (#05071e): figure and inner icons are solid white,
           and the wordmark is white.
  _mono    Single flat brand blue #1440a8. One-colour print (screen printing, stamps, embroidery).
  _black   Pure black. Black-and-white print, fax, engraving, newspaper.
  _white   Reversed (all white, figure cut out). On dark photos or coloured backgrounds.

COLOURS
  Cloud gradient   #1440a8 -> #1f74d1 -> #0aa8e0 -> #00c8ec
  Wordmark         #1440a8 -> #1f74d1 -> #0b8fd4
  Dark-mode dust   #3aa0f0
  Site background  #05071e

USAGE RULES
  - Keep clear space around the logo of at least the height of the "S" in SUSUME on every side.
  - Do not stretch, recolour outside these modes, add effects (shadows, glows) or put the colour
    version on busy photos (use _white instead).
  - Minimum sizes: primary 8 cm / 300 px wide, simplified 3 cm / 120 px, icon 48 px.
